ANECHOIC CHAMBER 57

A medium-length film and fashion collection of science fiction and surreal psychological drama, conceived together as a critique of an increasingly homogenized and repressed society. In this work, Ligeia explores concepts of psychoanalysis such as the repressed Jungian shadow and the search for authenticity in a world where everyone is the same.
